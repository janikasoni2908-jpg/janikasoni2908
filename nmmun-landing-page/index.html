Conference Legacy & Heritage

A Diplomatic Retreat in Maharashtra's Educational Oasis

Established in 2009, Narsee Monjee Model United Nations (NMMUN Shirpur) is the signature diplomatic conference organized by SVKM's NMIMS Shirpur Campus. Unlike metropolitan single-day conferences, NMMUN Shirpur offers a secluded, high-immersion diplomatic sanctuary where delegates live, dine, caucus, and negotiate around the clock in an extraordinary collegiate township.

Substantive Rigour & Adjudication

Led by nationally acclaimed Executive Boards from premier law and international relations circuits. Background guides, rules of procedure, and clause-by-clause scrutiny reflect the highest standards of the UN General Assembly and international tribunals.

24/7 Diplomatic Immersion

Because all delegates reside together in the on-campus suites, lobbying doesn't halt when committee adjourns. Alliances are solidified over late-night coffee in hostel courtyards, working papers are refined under the stars, and midnight crisis updates ignite real-time war cabinets.

All-Inclusive Campus Hospitality

Deluxe hostel accommodation, 4-meal daily multi-cuisine dining, airport shuttle convoys from Indore (IDR) & Bhusawal, and round-the-clock medical & security coverage create a seamless, safe, and stress-free retreat for outstation delegates.
